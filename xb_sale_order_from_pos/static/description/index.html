Sales, Quotations & Layaway from POS

Create quotations, orders and layaways at the till — collect advances, settle the balance, print an enriched receipt with an online-payment QR.

[ Banner / hero screenshot — POS with the “Order / Layaway” action ]

Built clean-room on native Odoo (point_of_sale, pos_sale, sale, l10n_mx_edi). It coexists with — does not replace — the native Quotation/Order loader. Every feature is optional and configured per Point of Sale.

🌎 Works with any localization. First-class support for Mexican CFDI 4.0 (advances / settlement invoicing, SAT payment forms, branch RFC). l10n_mx_edi ships as a dependency but stays dormant unless your company uses the Mexican localization — non-MX companies get pure native invoicing.

What it does

📝 Quotations, Orders & Layaways

Create the three document types straight from the POS. None is confirmed on creation — confirmation happens natively when the payment is validated.

💵 Multiple advances

Collect one or more partial advances (down payments) through the native down-payment product. The balance is payment-based and always up to date.

🧾 Enriched receipt

State-aware title, payment summary (Total / Advance / Balance), product detail on advance tickets, and a consistent cash-rounding line.

🔗 Online payment QR

While a balance remains, the receipt prints a portal QR so the customer can review and pay online. A configurable self-invoice QR is also supported.

Required setup (per selling company)

Configuration requirements learned in the field — the module attributes the invoice to the right company automatically, but the fiscal/accounting master data must exist on each selling company.

  1. Down-payment product (native POS) — set its income account for every selling company (on the product or its category). A branch without it fails the advance invoice with “Falta la cuenta requerida.”
  2. Settle rounding product (optional) — a 0%-IVA service with Available in POS = OFF (mandatory; if left visible it can break POS loading), an income account per company, and — in Mexico — a SAT ClaveProdServ (placeholder 01010101 if none applies). If unset, an all-16% settle simply tolerates a ±0.01 residual.
  3. POS payment method → SAT “Forma de pago” — map each method (e.g. MX: Cash → 01, Transfer → 03, Debit → 28, Credit → 04) so the CFDI reports the real payment form.
  4. Multi-company / branches — when branches share a sales journal, the invoice is attributed to the order’s company; the CFDI is issued under the branch RFC if it has one, otherwise under the nearest ancestor with a VAT (native l10n_mx_edi behaviour).
  5. Cash rounding — “only on cash” rounding is excluded from the CFDI automatically; the cash difference stays in the session. Intended behaviour.

Configuration

Settings ▸ Point of Sale ▸ “XUBAX – Sale Order / Quotation”. English source with an es_MX translation. A single master switch enables the feature; every sub-option below depends on it.

  • Create Sale Order / Quotation — master switch.
  • Default document state — which type is pre-selected by default (does not confirm anything on creation).
  • Allow quotations — let the cashier save a draft quotation.
  • Differentiate Order and Layaway — separate actions vs. one combined.
  • Show total & pending balance — in the POS order panel.
  • Detailed order receipt — product detail, total and balance.
  • Show customer reference — before the customer name.
  • Auto-print receipt on create.
  • Show online portal link on receipt — the payment QR (needs Detailed order receipt).
  • Self-invoice QR only when settled — avoids a double QR on advances (needs Self-Invoicing).
  • Settle rounding product — absorbs the rounding cent.

The receipt

[ Screenshot — advance ticket with balance + payment QR ]

Title by document state, a Total / Advance / Balance summary, the payment QR while a balance remains, a configurable self-invoice QR, the advance’s product detail, and a cash-rounding line that always reconciles (Total + Rounding = To Pay; Cash − To Pay = Change).

Note for implementers: installing over existing data recomputes amount_unpaid on POS-linked sale orders (a native-compute correction), which may surface a real pending balance on orders that previously looked paid.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19.0 Enterprise.
  • Depends: point_of_sale, pos_sale, sale, l10n_mx_edi (the latter a hard dependency that stays dormant on non-Mexican companies — keyed on the fiscal country, like l10n_mx_edi itself).
  • Soft dep: order-level internal note maps to sale.order.internal_note only when sale_subscription is present — guarded, no error otherwise.

Known limitations

  • Only a single rounding currency (MXN) tested.
  • SO header delivery_status stays empty — delivery is fulfilled through the POS picking, not an SO picking (by design).
  • No in-POS customer signature yet (roadmap).
