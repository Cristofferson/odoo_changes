Xibo Connector — Point of Sale

Thank-you messages, cart updates, customer display mirror and contextual upselling on Xibo screens — driven by your Odoo POS.

Four retail scenarios

1

Thank-you on paid order

Personalized greeting on the screen when the order is paid. Falls back to a generic message if the customer hasn't opted in.

2

Cart-add notifications

Overlay or ticker appears the moment a product is added to the cart, showing name, price and quantity.

3

Customer Display mirror

One-click setup: pick a screen and the connector auto-creates the Xibo layout and schedules it. The Odoo POS Customer Display appears on Xibo.

4

Contextual recommendationsNEW

Cashier adds a TV — the screen shows extended warranties, accessories, larger models. Driven by tags on product categories.

How contextual recommendations work

  1. Upload videos to your Xibo Library with descriptive tags (e.g. tv,electronics,accessories).
  2. Set the same tags on your Product Categories in Odoo.
  3. (Optional) Override on specific products that need different recommendations.
  4. Enable scenario ④ Contextual Recommendations on the POS configuration.
  5. As the cashier adds tagged products to the cart, matching Xibo media plays automatically on the screens.

A configurable cool-down (default 60 s) prevents screen spam. Choose how the system picks content when multiple tagged products are in the cart: latest added, most expensive, or accumulate all.

Privacy by design

Each contact has a "Show on Public Screens" toggle (default off). When disabled, the screen shows a generic message such as "valued customer" instead of the real name. Cashiers don't need to remember privacy preferences — the system handles it.

Setup in 3 minutes

  1. Install the base Xibo Connector module and configure your CMS server.
  2. Install this module.
  3. Open Point of Sale → Configuration → Settings and scroll to the Xibo Signage block.
  4. Pick your CMS server, default displays and the layouts/datasets per scenario.
  5. Save. Done.

Customer Display mirror — player compatibility

The Customer Display mirror works best on Xibo Player for Windows and Linux. Android, webOS and Tizen players have stricter cookie policies that may break the session-scoped Odoo Customer Display URL.
